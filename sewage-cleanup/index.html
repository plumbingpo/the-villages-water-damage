A sewage backup is one of the most hazardous situations a property owner can face. Pathogens in raw sewage include salmonella, giardia, hepatitis and E. coli, along with other viruses, parasites and bacteria, and contact with anything contaminated is a serious risk to health. The Villages Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout The Villages, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

How to Tell You Need Sewage Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Gurgling sounds from toilets or drains when other fixtures are used
  • Dark residue from sewage left on the basement floor or ringing a drain
  • Soggy, smelly ground over the sewer line or leakage at the cleanout cap
  • Wet spots on floors or walls accompanied by foul odors
  • Strong sewage or rotten egg odors coming from drains or lower levels
  • A toilet overflow containing waste that has spread onto flooring or carpet
  • Tubs, toilets or floor drains pushing black water back into the room

The Benefits of Professional Sewage Cleanup

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Contaminated materials count as biohazard waste, and disposing of them properly is part of a professional cleanup. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. A certified restoration company follows proper disposal protocols and works with licensed facilities that accept biohazard waste.

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Keep cooking oil, grease and food scraps out of the drain, where they build into clogs
  • Find your sewer cleanout now and check its condition, so it can be reached in an emergency
  • Flush nothing but toilet paper, because wipes, even 'flushable' ones, cause clogs
  • Fit a strainer over each sink and shower drain so hair and debris stay out of the pipes
  • Clear tree roots near sewer lines, which can infiltrate and block the pipes
  • Stormwater from a sump or downspout should never drain into the sewer, since the extra volume invites a backup
