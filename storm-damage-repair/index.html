Storm Damage Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Schedule a roof inspection every year so weak spots are repaired before storm season
  • Cut back limbs that hang over or close to the house so wind cannot throw them into it
  • Know how to shut off your main water supply in case a storm damages a pipe
  • Install impact-resistant windows or storm shutters if you live in a hurricane-prone area
  • Extend downspouts and slope the soil so runoff moves away from the foundation
  • Clogged gutters overflow in a downpour, so clear them and the downspouts often

Signs It Is Time for Storm Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Puddles near skylights, beside a chimney or on the attic floor
  • Downspouts knocked off and gutter runs hanging after high wind
  • Ceiling drywall that sags or bulges, or paint that bubbles after rain
  • Ceilings or walls that stain while it is storming or soon afterward
  • After a weather event, insulation inside walls or overhead feels soaked
  • Water in the lowest level after storm drains back up or overflow
  • Shingles that are gone, cracked or curled up when you look from the yard

Why Storm Damage Repair Is Not a DIY Job

Because one kind of storm damage feeds another, the work needs restoration professionals with real experience. A roof leak that looks minor from inside may have done extensive hidden damage to insulation, roof decking and wall cavities, and that leads to mold growth and structural deterioration if it is not found and dried. With moisture meters and thermal cameras, trained technicians follow the route water took and find all the areas it reached.

Emergency board-up and tarping also take proper technique to be effective. If a tarp is fastened poorly, it can direct water where you do not want it or give out when the wind picks up. Our crews are experienced in storm damage mitigation and use the right materials and methods to give reliable temporary protection while permanent repairs are scheduled.
