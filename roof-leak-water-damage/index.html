Roof Leak Water Damage Help in The Villages

The spot on your ceiling is seldom directly below the hole in the roof. Water enters at a raised shingle, a split in the flashing or a worn skylight seal, follows the rafters and decking downhill, and finally lands on the insulation. A ceiling stain tends to arrive late, well after the space overhead first got wet. We find the full wet area and dry it out.

We are a water damage crew, not a roofing company. We work on the interior, which means removing wet insulation, drying drywall and framing, dealing with mold and rebuilding the ceiling. When the roof is still letting water through, a temporary tarp is something we can install, and we will coordinate with your roofer so the repair comes before the ceiling is closed.

Do This First

Before We Arrive

  1. Shut Off Wet CircuitsIf water is dripping through a light fixture, a ceiling fan or near an outlet, switch off that circuit at the breaker and leave the fixture alone.
  2. Contain the WaterSet buckets or pans under active drips and move furniture, rugs and electronics out of the way. Lay towels or plastic sheeting down to protect the floor.
  3. Relieve a Ceiling BulgeIf the ceiling is holding a pocket of water, stand to one side and poke a small hole at the lowest point so it drains into a bucket.
  4. Leave the Roof AloneDo not climb onto a wet roof or crawl through a dark attic. From indoors, mark where the water enters, and let professionals handle everything else.
  5. Photograph the DamageUse your phone to record the damaged ceiling, attic and belongings first, hold off on throwing things out until that is done, and call us to get the drying started.
Coverage

What About Insurance?

Interior damage from a sudden, accidental roof leak, like one caused by a fallen limb or wind-torn shingles, is often covered under a homeowners policy. Insurers generally turn down claims tied to wear, age or lack of upkeep, and they may handle the roof repair on different terms than the inside damage. Photos and moisture readings from us go to the adjuster, though only your policy decides coverage, and your agent can explain it.

The Usual Culprits

How It Usually Starts

  • Fasteners working loose over time, so water follows the nail shank into the attic.
  • A valley liner that has worn through at the point where the most runoff passes.
  • Holes and fractures left in the roof after a branch or hailstones hit it.
  • Aging, cracked seals around a skylight frame that no longer keep water out.
  • Shingles that are cracked, curled or blown off by wind, exposing the underlayment.
Why Speed Matters

What Happens if You Wait

Attic Insulation

Once soaked, insulation flattens, loses its value and keeps the drywall under it damp for days. Drying is sometimes possible with fiberglass. Cellulose that is saturated generally comes out.

Ceiling Fixtures and Wiring

Because fixtures are holes in the drywall, a leak often shows up at a light or fan first. Keep that circuit off until someone has inspected it, since damp wiring may corrode or short out.

Ceiling Drywall

Drywall that stays wet stains, sags and loses its strength. Joints open, tape peels, and a waterlogged piece may fall without much notice.

Inside the Walls

Water that reaches the top of a wall runs down inside it, wetting insulation and the back of the drywall. Bubbling paint or a swollen baseboard may be the only sign.

What We Do

How We Handle Roof Leak Water Damage

1

Map Where Water Went

Using thermal imaging and moisture meters, our team checks wall cavities, ceilings and the attic and charts the whole route the water took, even where the room below shows nothing.

2

Cover and Protect

An exposed roof gets covered with a temporary tarp, or we line things up with your roofing contractor. We also cover floors and contents under the damaged ceiling.

3

Remove and Dry

Unsalvageable drywall and insulation come out, and dehumidifiers and air movers go into the attic space and each room under it, with readings taken until the framing has dried.

4

Sanitize and Repair

Where it is called for, we apply an antimicrobial to the bare framing. Once the roof repair is done, we replace insulation, hang and finish new drywall, then prime and paint to match the ceiling.
